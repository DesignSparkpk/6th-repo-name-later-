## Script

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "AI won't make you rich." then "...but it can make you FAST."]**
VO: Let's be honest. AI is not a magic money button. Anyone selling you "make ten thousand a month with zero effort" is selling you a course, not a business.

**[SCREEN: STOCK — "person working laptop cafe"]**
VO: But here's what AI *does* do: it lets one person do the work of three. That means you can offer real services faster and cheaper than before. So I've ranked five AI side hustles — from easiest to start, to highest potential — and for each one I'll tell you the tools, how to land your first client, and the downside nobody mentions.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "#5 AI-ASSISTED WRITING & EDITING"]**
VO: Number five: AI-assisted writing and editing. Businesses constantly need blog posts, product descriptions, newsletters and website copy.

**[SCREEN: REC — chatbot drafting product descriptions from a short brief]**
VO: You use an AI chatbot to create the first draft and do the research, then you — the human — edit it, fact-check it, and make it sound like the brand. That last part is the actual product. Clients don't pay for words; they pay for words that work.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "First client: Fiverr, Upwork, local businesses"]**
VO: How to get your first client: create a gig on a freelance marketplace, or message ten local businesses with a free rewrite of one page of their website. Show the before and after.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "⚠️ Downside"]**
VO: The downside: lots of competition at the cheap end. You win by picking a niche — say, writing only for dentists or only for real-estate agents — and becoming the specialist.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "#4 SHORT-FORM VIDEO EDITING"]**
VO: Number four: short-form video editing. Every business, coach and creator wants Shorts, Reels and TikToks — but they hate editing.

**[SCREEN: REC — CapCut auto-captions and auto-cut on a sample clip]**
VO: Tools like CapCut now do auto-captions, silence removal and even pick highlight clips from a long video automatically. What used to take an hour takes fifteen minutes. You sell packages: for example, twelve edited clips a month.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "First client: podcasters & coaches"]**
VO: First client: find podcasters with long episodes and no Shorts. Edit one clip for free from their latest episode and send it to them. That's the whole pitch.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "⚠️ Downside"]**
VO: The downside: it's a time-for-money business. Great to start, but you'll eventually need templates or helpers to grow.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "#3 DIGITAL PRODUCTS & TEMPLATES"]**
VO: Number three: digital products. Planners, spreadsheet templates, Notion dashboards, resume templates, prompt packs, printable worksheets.

**[SCREEN: REC — Canva designing a weekly planner page]**
VO: You use AI to brainstorm what people need and to draft the content, then you design it in Canva and sell it on platforms like Etsy, Gumroad or your own store. Make it once, sell it many times.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "Research = what already sells"]**
VO: The trick is research. Don't guess. Look at what's already selling well, find the reviews complaining about what's missing, and build the better version.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "⚠️ Downside"]**
VO: The downside: sales start slow. Your first month might be zero. It's a snowball — you need many products and patience.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "#2 AI CHATBOTS & AUTOMATION FOR SMALL BUSINESSES"]**
VO: Number two — and this is where the money gets serious: setting up AI chatbots and automations for small businesses.

**[SCREEN: AI-IMG — "small business owner's website with a friendly chat widget answering a customer, clean flat illustration, dark blue theme"]**
VO: Think about a dentist, a gym, a restaurant. They get the same questions every day: opening hours, prices, bookings. You set up a website chatbot that answers those questions, or an automation that sends booking reminders and follow-up emails automatically. Many no-code tools exist for this — no programming needed.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "Setup fee + monthly maintenance"]**
VO: You charge a setup fee, then a monthly fee to maintain and improve it. That monthly fee is what makes this powerful — recurring income.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "⚠️ Downside"]**
VO: The downside: you need to learn the tools properly, and you need to be comfortable talking to business owners. But once you've built it for one gym, you can build it for every gym.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "#1 FACELESS CONTENT"]**
VO: And number one: faceless content — like this exact channel.

**[SCREEN: REC — YouTube Studio analytics dashboard (blurred numbers)]**
VO: You pick a niche, use AI to help research and script, use AI voice and visuals, and publish consistently on YouTube, TikTok or Instagram. Once you grow, income comes from ads, sponsors, and affiliate links. And unlike client work, every video keeps earning while you sleep.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "⚠️ The biggest downside"]**
VO: The downside is time. It usually takes months of consistent posting before real income. And platforms are cracking down on low-effort, mass-produced AI videos. The channels that win add real value — real information, real opinions — and use AI as a tool, not a replacement for thinking.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — table "Fast cash: #5, #4 · Long-term: #3, #2, #1"]**
VO: So which one should you pick? If you need money soon, start with writing or video editing — you can land a client this week. If you're building for the long term, go with digital products, automation services, or content. And the best combo? Do one of each: a service that pays now, and an asset that grows over time.

**[SCREEN: TEXT — "Pick ONE. Start THIS weekend."]**
VO: Whatever you choose, pick one and start this weekend. Not next month. This weekend.

**[SCREEN: end screen]**
VO: And to do any of these well, you need to research fast. This next video shows you how to research anything ten times faster with AI. See you there.
