# Shorts batch 01 (S01–S15)

Each Short runs 25–45 seconds. **HOOK** is the on-screen text for the first second. Paste the **VO** into the voice tool, and use **Title** as both the Short's title and its caption.
Every Short gets these hashtags: `#AI #AItools #Shorts` plus the one listed.
Visuals: screen recordings of the action described, or text on a dark background with lime highlights.

---

### S01 — The one-line prompt fix
**HOOK:** Add this to EVERY AI prompt
**VO:** Add this one line to the end of every AI prompt: "Before you start, ask me any questions you need to do this well." Now instead of guessing, the AI interviews you first. You answer three or four questions, and the result is made for you, not for everyone. It's the single easiest upgrade to your AI answers. Save this.
**Title:** The one line that fixes bad AI answers #promptengineering

### S02 — Free second draft
**HOOK:** AI's first answer is never its best
**VO:** The first thing AI writes is never its best. After any answer, type: "Act as a strict editor. List the three biggest weaknesses, then rewrite it fixing them." The second version is almost always sharper, clearer and shorter. It's basically a free editor. Try it on your next email.
**Title:** Make AI edit itself (free second draft) #productivity

### S03 — Email in 20 seconds
**HOOK:** Writing emails? Stop.
**VO:** Stop writing long emails from scratch. Type your messy thoughts as bullet points, then say: "Turn this into a polite, clear email under 120 words. Keep my tone friendly but professional." Twenty seconds, done. And always read it before sending — you're still the boss.
**Title:** Write any email in 20 seconds with AI #emailtips

### S04 — Explain like I'm 12
**HOOK:** Understand ANY topic in 1 minute
**VO:** Confused by a topic? Paste it into any AI and say: "Explain this like I'm twelve, then give me one real-life example, then quiz me with three questions." Simple explanation, an example, and a test. That's how you actually learn, not just read.
**Title:** Learn anything faster with this AI prompt #studytips

### S05 — Meeting notes
**HOOK:** Never take meeting notes again
**VO:** Paste a meeting transcript or your rough notes into AI and say: "Give me a five-line summary, a list of decisions made, and a table of action items with who and when." Your whole meeting, organized in seconds. Just don't paste anything confidential your company doesn't allow.
**Title:** Turn messy meeting notes into action items with AI #worksmarter

### S06 — Resume booster
**HOOK:** Your resume is getting ignored. Here's why.
**VO:** Paste the job description and your resume into AI and ask: "Which keywords and skills from this job are missing from my resume? Rewrite my bullet points to show results with numbers — but only using facts I gave you." Tailored resume in five minutes. Never let it invent experience.
**Title:** Make your resume match any job with AI #jobsearch

### S07 — Role prompting
**HOOK:** Same AI. 2 totally different answers.
**VO:** Ask AI "how do I save money" and you get generic tips. Now ask: "You're a financial coach for students living on a small budget. Give me a realistic weekly plan." Completely different answer. Giving AI a role changes its vocabulary, depth and focus. Always start with a role.
**Title:** Why you should give AI a role #chatgpt

### S08 — Fact-check warning
**HOOK:** AI lied to me. Confidently.
**VO:** AI can invent statistics, quotes and sources that look completely real. So here's the rule: any number, date or quote you plan to use, click through to the original source and check it. Use AI search tools that show citations, and actually open them. Trust, but verify.
**Title:** Never trust AI numbers without this check #airesearch

### S09 — Weekly planner
**HOOK:** Let AI plan your week
**VO:** Tell AI your goals, your fixed commitments and how many hours you have free, then say: "Build me a realistic weekly schedule with breaks, and put the hardest task when I have the most energy — mornings." You'll get a plan in seconds. Then adjust it — it's a draft, not a prison.
**Title:** Plan your whole week in 30 seconds with AI #productivityhacks

### S10 — Side hustle reality
**HOOK:** The truth about AI side hustles
**VO:** AI won't make you rich overnight. But it lets one person do the work of three. That means you can offer writing, video editing or design services faster than before. The money isn't in the AI — it's in solving a real problem for a real customer, faster. Full breakdown on my channel.
**Title:** The honest truth about making money with AI #sidehustle

### S11 — Study with your notes
**HOOK:** Turn your notes into a personal tutor
**VO:** Upload your class notes or PDFs into an AI tool that answers from your files — like NotebookLM. Then ask: "Quiz me on chapter three, one question at a time, and explain my mistakes." It only uses your material, so it's focused on exactly what you need for the exam.
**Title:** Turn your notes into an AI tutor #studywithme

### S12 — Recipe from fridge
**HOOK:** What can I cook with THIS?
**VO:** Take a photo of your fridge or list what's in it, and ask AI: "Give me three dinner ideas using only these ingredients, under thirty minutes, with steps." No more staring at the fridge. No more wasted food.
**Title:** Let AI decide dinner from your fridge #lifehacks

### S13 — Examples beat adjectives
**HOOK:** Stop describing. Start showing.
**VO:** Instead of telling AI "make it catchy and fun," paste two examples you love and say "write a third one in exactly this style." AI is amazing at copying patterns. One good example beats ten adjectives. This is how pros get consistent results.
**Title:** The pro trick for consistent AI results #promptengineering

### S14 — Negotiation practice
**HOOK:** Practice your next salary talk with AI
**VO:** Tell AI: "Role-play as my manager. I'm asking for a raise. Push back realistically, then after five exchanges give me feedback on what I did well and what to improve." Practicing a hard conversation once makes the real one much easier.
**Title:** Practice hard conversations with AI role-play #careertips
