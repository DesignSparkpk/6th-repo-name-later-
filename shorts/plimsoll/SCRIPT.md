## Facts and sources
- Coffin ships were overloaded and over-insured, so owners profited even when they sank: 99% Invisible ep. 33; National Museums Liverpool; Wikipedia.
- 22 July 1875: Plimsoll shook his fist, called members "villains" and refused to withdraw the word when the Speaker asked;
  the papers printed his protest. Sheffield meeting quote: "If Mr Plimsoll is mad, the sooner he bit a few other MPs the better." (National Museums Liverpool)
- Disraeli announced the bill would not pass before the recess (Liverpool; Wikipedia). Disraeli moved a reprimand, adjourned; Plimsoll later apologised (Wikipedia).
- The 1876 Act made load lines compulsory, but owners set the line themselves; its position was fixed by law later
  (1890 or 1894 by source, so the script says only "years"): Liverpool; Royal Museums Greenwich.
- Plimsoll shoes were named in the 1920s for their resemblance to the line (Wikipedia).
- NOT used (unverified): suspension from Parliament, a line painted on the funnel, any death toll numbers.
