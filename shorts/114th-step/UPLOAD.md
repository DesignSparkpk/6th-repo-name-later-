## Script (spoken)
This lighthouse has one hundred and thirteen steps. Last night, it had one hundred and fourteen.
Every night for eleven years, the keeper climbed them, counting each one out loud. He never lost count.
Then a storm killed the power. Pitch black. He climbed by touch.
One hundred eleven. One hundred twelve. One hundred thirteen.
His foot found another step. One hundred fourteen.
He stopped. Below him, in the dark, someone kept counting. One hundred fifteen. One hundred sixteen.
In his voice.
He ran. The lamp at the top was already burning. And beside it sat a man with his face.
It looked up and smiled. "You're late. I've been climbing for you."
The ships swear the light never went out. But the keeper never came down.
So next time you climb stairs in the dark... count them.
