## Facts (with sources)
- **Vdara, Las Vegas:** opened 2009. Around 2010 its curved glass facade focused sun onto the pool deck: guests reported singed hair and melted plastic cups and shopping bags; staff called it the "Vdara death ray"; the hotel added bigger umbrellas. ([Techdirt](https://www.techdirt.com/2013/09/16/dailydirt-dangerous-death-ray-buildings/), [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/death-ray-holiday/))
- **Same architect:** both buildings were designed by Rafael Viñoly. He is not named or caricatured in the video. ([designboom](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/rafael-vinolys-20-fenchurch-skyscraper-is-melting-cars/), [Techdirt](https://www.techdirt.com/2013/09/16/dailydirt-dangerous-death-ray-buildings/))
- **20 Fenchurch Street ("Walkie-Talkie"), London, 2013:** the concave south face melted a parked Jaguar XJ's wing mirror, badge and panels; paint blistered and a carpet was scorched in nearby shops; parking bays were suspended. ([National Geographic](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/article/130904-walkie-talkie-building-london-melts-sunlight-physics-science), [ITV](https://www.itv.com/news/update/2013-09-03/glare-from-walkie-talkie-skyscraper-melts-jaguar/), [world-architects](https://world-architects.com/en/pages/headlines/walkie-talkie-building-melts-cars))
- **The egg:** a City A.M. reporter fried an egg in the beam. ([City A.M.](https://www.cityam.com/?p=550958))
- **Physics:** the hot spot got about 10-15 times the solar heat flux of direct sunlight. ([Pop Sci](https://www.popsci.com/science/article/2013-09/why-do-skyscrapers-melt-things/))
- **Fix:** a brise soleil sunshade was fitted in 2014. ([Construction Index](https://www.theconstructionindex.co.uk/news/view/walkie-scorchie-sun-shade-goes-up-this-month?amp=1), [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fryscraper))
- **Punchline quote:** "When I first came to London years ago, it wasn't like this. Now you have all these sunny days." ([Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2013/09/06/we-made-a-lot-of-mistakes-with-this-building-says-walkie-scorchie-architect-vinoly/))
