## Weekly check (YouTube Studio → Analytics)
Note these numbers for every video:
- **Impressions click-through rate:** below 4% means change the thumbnail or title. Above 7% means make more videos like it.
- **Average percentage viewed:** below 35% means the first 30 seconds lose people, so make hooks shorter.
- **Shorts "viewed vs swiped away":** below 60% viewed means the first-second hook is weak.
- **Subscribers gained per video:** shows which topics to repeat.

## Rules
1. Never miss a scheduled post during the first 90 days.
2. In week 3, take your best-performing video and make 3 follow-ups on the same topic from different angles.
3. Reply to every comment in the first hour after posting.
4. Pin a question comment on every video.
