# 90-day Shorts plan

## Posting schedule
- **2 Shorts a day** in weeks 1–4, then **3 a day** from week 5 if you're keeping up.
- Space them 6–8 hours apart. Use YouTube Studio's **Schedule** option so you can upload a whole week in one sitting.
- Good posting times for a US and global English audience are 15:00, 19:00 and 23:00 UTC (8 pm, midnight and 4 am Pakistan time). Scheduling handles this for you.

## Targets (realistic, not guaranteed)
| By | Total views | Subscribers |
|---|---|---|
| Day 30 | 50k–300k | 100–600 |
| Day 60 | 300k–1.5M | 500–2,000 |
| Day 90 | 1M–5M+ | 1,000–5,000 |

Most Shorts get a few hundred views. Growth comes from the occasional one that takes off, so posting volume and consistency matter most.

## Weekly check (YouTube Studio → Content → Shorts)
For each Short, note:
1. **Viewed vs swiped away**: above 70% is great, below 55% means the hook needs work.
2. **Average percentage viewed**: above 90% is great (viewers rewatch the loop).
3. **Subscribers gained**.

Send the top 3 and bottom 3 Shorts to Claude. The next batch will repeat the winning topics and hook styles and drop the losers.

## What to repeat once something works
- When a Short gets 10 times your usual views, make **3 follow-ups** on the same idea within a week (for example "Part 2", "3 more prompts like this", "this but for students").
- Turn your best-performing topic into a **series** with a consistent title, such as "AI Shortcut #12".
