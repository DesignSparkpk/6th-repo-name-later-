# What's working in AI-tips Shorts (research, Nov 2026)

**Method:** I pulled titles and view counts from YouTube's Shorts search results for 12 searches in the niche, such as "chatgpt hacks", "claude ai tips", "ai tools you need", "ai for excel", "notebooklm" and "ai tool that feels illegal". I looked at cover frames of the top and bottom performers and added published retention research.
**Limits:** vidIQ had no credits left, and YouTube rate-limited the video pages, so I couldn't read full transcripts or second-by-second retention data. These conclusions come from titles, view counts, cover frames and the research listed at the end.

## 1. The data

### Winners (millions of views)
| Views | Title | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 10M | AI Tools for every task in 2026 | **Huge, save-worthy list.** You can't take it all in at once, so people save and rewatch. |
| 5M | The ChatGPT Prompt That Turns It Into a Ruthless Mentor 👇 | **One** prompt with a **personality twist** and an emotional promise |

### Losers (single-digit to low hundreds of views)
- "3 FREE AI Tools You Need to Know! 🤯" got 5 views; "5 FREE AI Tools You NEED to Know in 2026!" got 54.
- **Ten different** "X AI Tools That Feel Illegal to Know" Shorts got between 5 and 1,338 views.
- "NotebookLM Renamed!" got 485; "Google NotebookLM Explained in 60 Seconds" got 197. News and explainers with no payoff do poorly.

The losers' cover frames are polished dark cards, generic AI-art backgrounds and tool logos. **That is very close to the style of our version 1 Shorts**, which is why version 1 is being replaced.

## 2. What the winners do

### The hook (0–2 seconds): conflict or a secret, not a topic
| ❌ Topic hook (dies) | ✅ Tension hook (works) |
|---|---|
| "5 AI tools you need" | "I tricked ChatGPT in 15 seconds" |
| "Tips for better prompts" | "This prompt turns AI into a ruthless mentor" |
| "AI for studying" | "95% of students use AI wrong" |
| "Cool AI feature" | "There's a hidden ___ in ___" |

Rules:
- The **first spoken line is the hook**. No intro, logo or "hey guys".
- The **on-screen text matches the spoken hook**, so it works with the sound off.
- The **first frame already shows the thing**, usually a real chat screen, not a title card.

### The rehook (around 40–60% of the way through)
Just as viewers think they've got it, open a new loop: "But that's not even the crazy part…", "Now watch what happens when I push back…", "Most people stop here. Don't." This is where swipe-aways usually happen, so give viewers a new reason to stay.

### The result (the payoff)
- **Show the actual output on screen.** The winners show real chat screens, not descriptions of them.
- One clear before/after, or one "watch this" moment.
- A reaction beat (an emoji or sound effect) at the payoff.

### The ending: a loop, not a goodbye
- The last line runs straight into the first line, so the replay feels seamless and the percentage-viewed number goes up.
- No "thanks for watching", and only a short "follow for part 2" when there really is a part 2.
- A **save-bait** element (a long list or a template) brings people back to rewatch.

### Length
- Single-trick Shorts: **15–30 seconds**. Lists: **30–50 seconds**, scrolled fast enough that people pause or save.

### What keeps viewers watching
1. **Something changes on screen every 1–2 seconds**: new caption words, a zoom, a new message bubble.
2. **Captions word by word**, big, centered, with the current word highlighted.
3. **Personality.** The winners have an attitude ("ruthless", "trap", "💀"). Our voice has to sound like a person with opinions, not a narrator.
4. **One audience per Short** (students, office workers, freelancers).
5. **Series**, such as "Part 2" or "Prompt #7", which bring viewers back.

## 3. What this means for our version 2 Shorts
| Version 1 (being replaced) | Version 2 |
|---|---|
| Silent | Kokoro voice with personality, word-by-word captions synced to it |
| Title-card first frame | First frame is a real chat screen with the hook text |
| Same structure every time | 6 formats: Trap, One Prompt, Mega List, Before→After, Myth Bust, Real Work Demo |
| Polished dark "AI aesthetic" | Native look: light chat interface, bold white captions with a black outline |
| Generic illustrative AI replies | Real AI outputs (Claude actually answers each prompt) |
| "Save this, follow" ending | Loop ending plus a series number |
